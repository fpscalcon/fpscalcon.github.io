I am a Research Associate in the Department of Electrical and Software Engineering at the University of Calgary, where I lead the technical direction of a campus microgrid research project, coordinating efforts across electrical and chemical engineering groups and supervising 2 PhD and 2 MSc students. My research focuses on the control of power electronic converters, distributed energy resources, and electromechanical energy conversion.

I hold a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the Federal University of Santa Maria (Brazil), where I developed advanced control strategies for switched reluctance motors. I have over 10 years of engineering experience spanning academic research and industry, including over 3 years at the postdoctoral level at the University of Calgary and McMaster University's Automotive Resource Centre (MARC), in collaboration with industrial partners such as TC Energy, Lockheed Martin, Enedym Inc., and Cubic Transportation Systems.

## Research Interests
- Control of power electronic converters and electric drives
- Switched reluctance machines and synchronous reluctance machines
- Microgrids and distributed energy resources
- Sliding mode control, optimization, and sensorless control strategies
